# Paid Time Off

## Allowance
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year, plus Korean public holidays. Leave accrues monthly, and up to 5 unused days can be carried over into the first quarter of the next year.

## Requesting leave
Leave of 3 days or more must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance in the HR portal. Engineers on the on-call rotation must arrange a swap before their leave is approved.

## Sick leave
Sick leave is separate from annual leave: employees get up to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
