# Customer Refunds

## Eligibility
Customers can get a full refund for a booking cancelled at least 24 hours before pickup. Cancellations within 24 hours of pickup are refunded at 50%. No refund is given after the carrier has collected the parcel.

## Lost parcels
If a parcel is confirmed lost by the carrier, the customer receives a full refund plus declared value up to 500,000 KRW, unless they bought extended insurance.

## Processing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Support agents can approve refunds up to 200,000 KRW; larger refunds need a team lead.
