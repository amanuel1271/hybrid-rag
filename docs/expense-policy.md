# Expense Policy

## Equipment
Each employee has an equipment budget of 1,500,000 KRW every two years for items such as monitors, keyboards and chairs. Laptops are provided by IT and are not part of this budget.

## Learning
Engineers can spend up to 1,000,000 KRW per year on books, courses and conference tickets. Conference travel needs manager approval in advance.

## Claims
Expenses must be submitted within 60 days with a receipt. Claims above 300,000 KRW need approval from a director. Meals during business travel are covered up to 50,000 KRW per day.
